game_analysis: The main point reports or explains sports events, records, tactics, equipment, or performance through concrete observations, examples, or mechanisms, without primarily prescribing a rule or personal recommendation.
fan_take: The main point is a personal judgment, prediction, recommendation, anecdote, joke, or speculative explanation rather than a rule decision or concrete factual account.
